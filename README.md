## 🌍 Who We Are: A Startup That Bets on Youth

**NeuronaX S.A.S.** is a proudly global startup founded on a bold conviction: **the ongoing Artificial Intelligence revolution belongs to young minds**.

Current labor market data shows that hundreds of thousands of talented computer science students and recent university graduates are systematically rejected by traditional enterprises under the label of "lacking senior experience." **At NeuronaX, we invert this paradigm.**

Led by **Alejandro Ortiz** (Chief Technology Officer & Computer Science student at University of the People), our human capital strategy actively recruits, mentors, and empowers students and juniors. We believe that genuine innovation in the generative AI era does not stem from outdated corporate bureaucracy, but from:
* 🚀 **Unrestricted creativity & out-of-the-box thinking**.
* 🧠 **Native fluency with foundational models and rapid prototyping**.
* 🤝 **High empathy, soft skills, and relentless curiosity**.

> **🎯 Our Vision:** To bring applied artificial intelligence to every sector, business, and school that still doesn't know how to unlock its power, delivering accessible, high-impact solutions with world-class craft.
